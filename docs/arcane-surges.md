# Arcane surges — locked v4

Locked on 2026-09-24. The authored source is `data/arcane-surges.js`.

## Table contract

- **Normal:** all 60 original normal results plus all 19 former critical/desperate results, preserved verbatim. This includes the temporarily petrified ally and the old demigod, black-hole, and planar-rift results.
- **Desperate:** exactly 25 authored outcomes. Entries 1–11 retain the accepted heavy concepts; entries 12–25 expand that set at the same scale.
- Keep the existing roll behavior: an unchecked roll has a 10% chance to use the desperate table. Checking **Desperate** guarantees that table. Entries within each pool are equally likely.
- The set is fixed for now. Do not silently add, soften, rebalance, or regenerate these entries. Future content changes require a new user request.
- v4 shortens every desperate description, including all storm and Aspect variants. Keep the effects, limits, and consequences intact; favor two compact paragraphs with one clear image and usable table rules.
- A desperate result should substantially change the encounter, location, stakes, or relationships. Permanent consequences, tremendous power, dangerous bargains, and campaign-changing events belong here.
- Favor recognizable Warcraft powers, beings, and mysteries: the world-soul, loa, Titans, Dragon Aspects, Old Gods, the Dream, the Legion, and the Light. Their arrival or borrowed power should matter immediately in combat or roleplay. The Titan result deliberately leaves its speaker and purpose unexplained.
- Avoid long jumps into the future, erased identities, invented shared lives, and shared life-force damage pools. The v3 replacements occupy the same nine numbered positions as the v2 replacements.
- **Elemental storm stirs** automatically selects wind, fire, ice, or sand with equal probability. **You wield a Dragon Aspect's power** automatically selects one of the five traditional domains: life, magic, dream, time, or earth. These are two entries in the 25-result pool; their variants do not increase the chance of rolling those entries. A selected variant stays fixed through its reveal.
- The surge events and mechanics are campaign homebrew inspired by Warcraft lore. They are not claims that these events happen in canon. Choose loa and Old God references to fit the campaign's region and era; the Old God result does not establish that a canonically defeated being has returned.
- Save DCs in the new desperate entries use the caster's spell save DC, or 15 if there is none. The result displays that rule whenever a save is required.
- If used outside initiative, a round is 6 seconds; the DM can start initiative when a countdown or competing actions matter. The text is a tabletop ruling aid, not an automated combat engine.

## Display behavior

The existing minimal controls and large result field remain. Desperate results use a large uppercase title and two readable paragraphs. Long results scroll within the result field, including on small screens.

A desperate roll has one 4.6-second sequence: a full-height vertical white line fades into view without growing taller, then opens horizontally from its center in 276 milliseconds to fill the result field with opaque white light. The light holds briefly before fading to the result. No title, shockwave, or other text appears during the buildup. Normal rolls retain the white reveal. No audio is added. **Reveal now** or Escape immediately reveals the already-selected result without drawing again. Keyboard-triggered rolls and reduced-motion preferences reveal immediately. Leaving the panel or hiding the browser finishes pending reveals and clears their timers.
